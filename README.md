# StudyMate — Personal Study Planner

**A smarter, calmer way to prepare for exams.**

StudyMate is a personal study planning web application designed to help learners organize their revision, prioritize difficult topics, and manage their study time before examinations.

## Problem Statement

Many students struggle to create realistic study timetables before exams. They often spend too much time on familiar topics while neglecting weaker areas or fail to distribute their revision time effectively.

StudyMate aims to make exam preparation more organized, manageable, and personalized.

## Proposed Solution

StudyMate helps learners build a personalized study plan based on their examination date, subjects, weak topics, and available study hours.

Instead of relying on a generic timetable, the application focuses on helping each learner understand what to study, when to study, and what needs more attention.

## Key Features

- **Personalized Study Planning:** Organize revision around examination dates and available study hours.
- **Subject and Topic Management:** Add subjects, organize topics, and identify areas that need improvement.
- **Weak Topic Prioritization:** Give challenging topics more attention during revision.
- **Study Timetable:** Plan study sessions and manage daily revision.
- **Progress Tracking:** Monitor completed sessions and learning progress.
- **Flexible Scheduling:** Adjust study sessions when plans change.
- **Data Persistence:** Save study information locally, where supported by the implementation.
